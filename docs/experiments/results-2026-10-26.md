### Experiment 6 in detail

- Device types exercised (every product passed, and each entity's probe went both ways): Color Temperature Light, Contact Sensor, Dimmable Light, Dimmable Plug-in Unit, Door Lock, Extended Color Light, Mounted Dimmable Load Control, Mounted On/Off Control, Occupancy Sensor, On/Off Light, On/Off Plug-in Unit, Room Air Conditioner, Temperature Sensor, Thermostat.
- Displayed only, as pre-registered (no stimulus yet): Air Quality Sensor, Rain Sensor, Smoke CO Alarm, Water Leak Detector.
- By category: light 4/4, plug 4/4, thermostat 2/2, leak sensor 2/2, lock, contact, motion, climate, smoke and air-quality sensor 1/1 each.
- Finding for the catalog: for two color-temperature lights (matter:1460:aa61, matter:1387:70c9), the twin's starting `ColorTemperatureMireds` (0) and `CoupleColorTempToLevelMinMireds` (1) lie below the product's physical minimum (135 mireds). The node refused them and the twins took the node's 250 and 153. Starting values should respect the product's physical limits.
- Occupancy clears after 90 s, an assumed timeout, for matter:1359:06ce.
